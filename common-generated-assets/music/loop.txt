Instrumental only, no vocals. A driving 1960s-inspired spy-heist thriller cue with a relentless infiltration feel, built for a seamless 30-second background loop under an anime prison-escape game.
Percussion-forward: tight taiko and tom ostinato in 5/4, crisp snare rolls, rimshots, shakers and low frame drums locked to a steady 120 BPM pulse. Staccato low strings and a pulsing synth bass play a sneaky, tense motif in a minor key; short brass stabs and plucked pizzicato accents answer on the off-beats; a subtle ticking hi-hat keeps urgency.
High energy but controlled and mixed for long listening: no screeching or piercing highs, no sudden huge drops, no big climactic ending, consistent intensity from start to finish, warm and punchy rather than harsh. The final bar should lead naturally back into the first bar so it loops cleanly.
