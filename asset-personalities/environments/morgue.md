# morgue
Prison morgue, steel drawers, a gurney, a zipped body bag tagged for collection.

**Security:** low · **Steps to nearest exit:** 1 · **Neighbors:** infirmary, utility-tunnels, loading-dock

## Personality
Cold, tiled, and almost never occupied. The prison would rather not think about this room, so it is checked on a
schedule rather than watched. Its one remarkable property: it is the only place in the building from which a
person is expected to leave the grounds without a headcount.

## Guards present
None posted · Officer Walt Finch unlocks it for collections

## Inmates present
None living

## Useful items
- body bags and toe tags
- wheeled gurney
- refrigerated drawers
- collection paperwork and clipboard
- surgical scissors and shroud linen

## Hazards
- hypothermia in a drawer
- the bag is zipped and counted from the outside
- collection times are fixed; miss one and there is not another
- discovery here is instantly understood for what it is

## Ways in and out
- Double doors → infirmary
- Floor hatch → utility-tunnels
- Collection corridor → loading-dock (where the hearse backs in)

## Clever ideas that should score high innovation
- Take the place of a body already tagged for collection
- Switch a toe tag so the paperwork matches the wrong body
- Use the gurney to move through the collection corridor unchallenged
- Hide in a drawer through a sweep, accepting the cold

## What gets Sloppy Joe caught (or killed)
- Freezing in a drawer while waiting too long
- Being in the bag when it is weighed or opened
- Missing the collection and being locked in overnight
