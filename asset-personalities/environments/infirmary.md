# infirmary
Sterile infirmary, curtained beds, medicine cabinet, barred windows.

**Security:** low · **Steps to nearest exit:** 2 · **Neighbors:** hall-main, air-vents

## Personality
Sterile, quiet, deceptively lightly guarded. A place people get sent.

## Guards present
One nurse, Officer Pratt sometimes escorts

## Inmates present
Sick inmates behind curtains

## Useful items
- sedatives
- scrubs
- gurney
- body bag
- IV tubing
- ceiling vent

## Hazards
- locked medicine cabinet
- nurse call button

## Ways in and out
- Door → hall-main
- Ceiling vent above a bed → air-vents

## Clever ideas that should score high innovation
- Fake illness to get sent here
- Wear scrubs as disguise
- Sedate a guard's coffee
- Hide in a body bag for transport (risky, dark)

## What gets Larry caught (or killed)
- Nurse presses the call button
- Sedatives go missing and get counted
