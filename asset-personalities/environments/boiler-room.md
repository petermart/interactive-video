# boiler-room
Boiler room, twin furnaces, iron catwalks, an ash chute in the far wall. Heat haze.

**Security:** low · **Steps to nearest exit:** 2 · **Neighbors:** utility-tunnels, laundry, workshop

## Personality
Deafening, furnace-hot, and permanently half-dark. Conversation is impossible over the burners, which cuts both
ways: nobody hears Sloppy Joe, and Sloppy Joe hears nobody coming. The building's heat and hot water start here,
so anything that goes wrong in this room is felt everywhere else within minutes.

## Guards present
Officer Walt Finch rarely (checks the gauges, never lingers)

## Inmates present
One work-detail stoker on rotation

## Useful items
- ash chute to the outer wall (narrow, filthy)
- furnace rake and shovel
- pressure gauges and relief valves
- asbestos gloves and work apron
- coal-dust sacking

## Hazards
- burns and scalding
- carbon monoxide in the flue
- the chute is a one-way commitment
- no sound carries out, so no one comes if it goes wrong

## Ways in and out
- Catwalk stair → utility-tunnels
- Service door → laundry
- Pipe gallery → workshop
- Ash chute: leads to the outer wall bins, too narrow to climb back

## Clever ideas that should score high innovation
- Trip the relief valve to lose the whole wing's hot water and pull guards away
- Ride the ash chute out to the wall bins (filthy, painful, unwatched)
- Use the asbestos gloves to handle something that should be untouchable
- Burn evidence in the furnace before a shakedown

## What gets Sloppy Joe caught (or killed)
- Overpressuring a boiler while standing next to it
- Getting wedged in the ash chute with no way back
- Passing out in the flue gas
