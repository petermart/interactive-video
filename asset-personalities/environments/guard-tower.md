# guard-tower
Tall concrete guard tower at the corner of the yard and perimeter fence: spiral stairs, a glass-walled lookout cabin with a rotating searchlight, rifle rack, radio console, and a catwalk ladder over the fence line.

**Security:** extreme · **Steps to nearest exit:** 1 · **Neighbors:** yard, perimeter-fence

## Personality
The all-seeing eye. Whoever holds the tower owns the yard and the fence. High risk, high reward.

## Guards present
Hawk (sniper, always in the cabin), shift-change relief guard at midnight

## Inmates present
None

## Useful items
- rotating searchlight (can be aimed or smashed)
- radio console (broadcast fake alerts)
- rifle rack (locked)
- binoculars
- catwalk ladder over the fence
- spare guard jacket on a hook

## Hazards
- Hawk's rifle
- climbing exposed in the searchlight
- long fall from the ladder
- the cabin door locks from inside

## Ways in and out
- Stairwell door at the base → yard
- Exterior ladder (exposed, but avoids the stairwell)
- Catwalk over the fence line → perimeter-fence (EXIT route)

## Clever ideas that should score high innovation
- Sneak up during the midnight shift change, disguised as the relief guard
- Swing the searchlight away from the fence, or smash it to black out that side
- Broadcast a fake alarm from another wing on the radio console
- Use the catwalk to drop past the razor wire

## What gets Sloppy Joe caught (or killed)
- Climbing the ladder while Hawk is watching (usually fatal)
- Fighting Hawk head-on in the cabin
