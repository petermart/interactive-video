# cell-block-a
The protagonist's cramped cell in Block A: steel bunk, sink, barred door facing a multi-tier cell block.

**Security:** medium · **Steps to nearest exit:** 2 · **Neighbors:** cell-block-tier, hall-main, air-vents

## Personality
Claustrophobic, familiar, the starting cage. Quiet at night except snoring and dripping pipes.

## Guards present
Walt Finch (night rounds), Sergeant Hollis (morning count)

## Inmates present
Big Oso (neighboring cell), Wren (upper tier)

## Useful items
- sharpened spoon
- bedsheets
- thin mattress
- vent grate screws
- tally-mark wall

## Hazards
- bars
- headcount every 2 hours
- creaky bunk

## Ways in and out
- Barred cell door → hall-main (needs key, trick or bribe)
- Vent grate high on the back wall → air-vents (climb the bunk bed; needs spoon/screwdriver)
- Stairs → cell-block-tier (once door is open)

## Clever ideas that should score high innovation
- Pillow decoy in bed to beat headcount
- Unscrew the vent with the spoon
- Fake illness to get escorted out
- Signal Wren or Oso through the wall

## What gets Sloppy Joe caught (or killed)
- Missing at headcount without a decoy
- Loud banging on the vent
