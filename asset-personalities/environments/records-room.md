# records-room
Records room, filing cabinets to the ceiling, prison blueprints, a key cabinet behind wired glass.

**Security:** high · **Steps to nearest exit:** 4 · **Neighbors:** warden-office, hall-main, chapel

## Personality
Administration, not incarceration: carpet tiles, a desk lamp, the smell of old paper. No inmate has any business
here, which means being caught is damning on its own. It is also the only room that knows the shape of the whole
building, and the room where an escape stops being improvised.

## Guards present
Deputy Warden Alistair Crane (works here) · Lieutenant Vera Cross reviews files

## Inmates present
None, ever

## Useful items
- prison blueprints and tunnel schematics
- key cabinet behind wired glass
- shift rosters and patrol timetables
- inmate files and transfer paperwork
- typewriter, stamps, headed paper
- a telephone with an outside line

## Hazards
- being here at all is unexplainable
- Crane notices anything moved
- the key cabinet is alarmed
- the door locks from the corridor side

## Ways in and out
- Connecting door → warden-office
- Main corridor → hall-main
- Vestry passage → chapel

## Clever ideas that should score high innovation
- Read the blueprints for a route that later steps can actually use
- Forge a transfer order so a van manifest expects a body
- Memorise the patrol timetable instead of stealing it, leaving nothing missing
- Take a key impression rather than the key, so nothing is noticed gone

## What gets Sloppy Joe caught (or killed)
- Triggering the key cabinet alarm
- Leaving the files visibly disturbed for Crane to find
- Being in the room when the corridor door locks
- Using the outside line, which is logged
