# motor-pool
Prison motor pool, transport vans over inspection pits, fuel drums, roll-up door to the yard road. Exit candidate.

**Security:** high · **Steps to nearest exit:** 0 (this is an exit) · **Neighbors:** loading-dock, yard, workshop

## Personality
The only room in the prison where something leaves the grounds under its own power every single day. Everyone
knows it, so the vans are searched, the manifests are counted, and the roll-up door is watched. The way out of
here is not the door: it is being inside something that is already allowed through it.

## Guards present
Officer Gus Donnelly (holds the vehicle keys) · Officer Dana Reyes & Kaiser sweep departures

## Inmates present
Two mechanics on work detail, resentful of supervision

## Useful items
- transport vans and a prison bus
- inspection pits beneath the vehicles
- fuel drums and siphon hose
- wheel wells and undercarriage frames
- trolley jack, wrenches, engine hoist
- clipboard of departure manifests

## Hazards
- Kaiser alerts on a person in a vehicle
- departure manifests are counted against bodies
- the pits are an obvious hiding place and get checked
- fuel and sparks

## Ways in and out
- Bay door → loading-dock
- Side gate → yard
- Pipe gallery → workshop
- Roll-up door → the yard road and out (watched, counted, dog-swept)

## Clever ideas that should score high innovation
- Ride the undercarriage rather than the cargo space, where the dog is not looking
- Alter the manifest so the count of bodies leaving is already correct
- Mask scent with fuel or grease so Kaiser passes the vehicle
- Use the inspection pit to move under a van mid-search rather than hide in it

## What gets Sloppy Joe caught (or killed)
- Hiding in the obvious cargo space
- Getting caught by Kaiser on the departure sweep
- Being crushed under a van when the jack slips
- Taking a vehicle by force, which brings the towers down on the road
