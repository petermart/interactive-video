# chapel
Prison chapel, worn pews, dusty stained glass, a padlocked organ loft above.

**Security:** low · **Steps to nearest exit:** 3 · **Neighbors:** hall-main, library, records-room

## Personality
The one room the prison pretends is not a prison. Nobody raises their voice. Guards give it a wide berth out of
habit rather than rule, which makes it the best place in the building to be seen doing nothing suspicious.

## Guards present
Officer Timmy Pratt occasionally (uncomfortable, does not search anyone)

## Inmates present
A handful, quiet, unwilling to be involved in anything

## Useful items
- candle stubs and matches
- heavy brass candlesticks
- hymn books (paper, hollowable)
- the padlocked organ loft ladder
- a collection box with a thin metal lip

## Hazards
- nowhere to hide if a count is called
- inmates who will not lie for you
- the loft ladder is loud

## Ways in and out
- Main doors → hall-main
- Side passage → library
- Vestry door → records-room
- Organ loft: overlooks the hall, no exit of its own

## Clever ideas that should score high innovation
- Use the service as cover for a meeting or a handoff
- Hollow a hymn book to move a tool between locations
- Wait out a patrol sweep in plain sight
- Pick the loft padlock to watch hall-main traffic unseen

## What gets Sloppy Joe caught (or killed)
- Being found in the vestry, which has no innocent explanation
- Trying to force the loft during a service
- Trusting an inmate who wants nothing to do with an escape
