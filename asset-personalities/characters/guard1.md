# Sergeant Brick Hollis
id: `guard-01` · Guard: veteran sergeant

## Personality
By-the-book, 30 years on the job, proud and unbending. Believes no one has ever escaped on his watch.

## Wants
Order, respect, and to retire with a spotless record.

## Weakness
Pride. Challenge his authority or make him look foolish and he overreacts, leaving his post to deal with it personally. Bad knees: slow on stairs.

## Usually found in
- hall-main
- yard
- cell-block-tier

## How they can help Sloppy Joe
Never willingly. His keys and radio are valuable if he is lured away.

## How Sloppy Joe can exploit or win them over
- Staged fights draw him in (he always responds himself).
- Can't chase up stairs or ladders quickly.

## Danger
Very strong; direct confrontation almost always fails.

## Voice
Booming, gravelly, barks orders.
