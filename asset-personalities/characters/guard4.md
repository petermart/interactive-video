# Officer Gus Donnelly
id: `guard-04` · Guard: key keeper

## Personality
Lazy, friendly, snack-obsessed, gossips with inmates. Carries the master key ring.

## Wants
Food, naps, and an easy shift.

## Weakness
Falls asleep after lunch, can be bribed with food, the key ring jangles loudly and is easy to spot on his belt.

## Usually found in
- cafeteria
- kitchen
- guard-station

## How they can help Sloppy Joe
Unknowingly: his keys open most interior doors.

## How Sloppy Joe can exploit or win them over
- Pickpocketing keys while he dozes.
- Bribing with kitchen desserts.
- Distracting him with gossip.

## Danger
Stealing the whole ring gets noticed fast (it goes silent). Swap it or take one key.

## Voice
Warm, wheezy, chuckles a lot.
