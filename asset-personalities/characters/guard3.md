# Lieutenant Vera Cross
id: `guard-03` · Guard: lieutenant

## Personality
Sharp, calm, observant. Reads people instantly. The most dangerous mind in the building.

## Wants
To catch the escape attempt she knows is coming.

## Weakness
Overconfident in the camera system. Trusts what the monitors show her.

## How they can help Larry
Never. But her radio calls reveal patrol routes if overheard.

## How Larry can exploit or win them over

- Overheard radio chatter gives timing windows.

Sees through simple lies and bluffs. Distractions near her usually fail.
