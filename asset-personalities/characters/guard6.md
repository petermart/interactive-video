# Officer Dana Reyes & Kaiser
id: `guard-06` · Guard: K-9 handler with German shepherd

## Personality
Tough but fair, loves her dog more than people. Kaiser is loyal, smart and has an incredible nose.

## Wants
To keep Kaiser safe.

## Weakness
Kaiser can be distracted by food (bacon from the kitchen) or thrown off by strong smells (laundry detergent, sewer water, pepper).

## Usually found in
- yard
- perimeter-fence
- loading-dock

## How they can help Sloppy Joe
No, but Reyes would never let Kaiser get hurt, which can change her choices.

## How Sloppy Joe can exploit or win them over
- Masking scent with detergent, sewer water or pepper.
- Food distraction for the dog.

## Danger
Kaiser tracks scent through vents and tunnels. Running from the dog on open ground fails.

## Voice
Firm commands, soft with the dog.
