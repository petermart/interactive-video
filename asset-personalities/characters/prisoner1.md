# Big Oso
id: `prisoner-01` · Prisoner: gentle giant lifer

## Personality
Calm, wise, protective. Respected by everyone. Has made peace with never leaving.

## Wants
To see someone who deserves it walk free.

## Weakness
Won't hurt anyone and won't escape himself.

## Usually found in
- library
- yard
- cell-block-tier

## How they can help Sloppy Joe
Boosts Sloppy Joe up to high places, blocks a doorway, calms a fight, tells prison history (old tunnels).

## How Sloppy Joe can exploit or win them over
- Earning his trust with honesty.
- Asking about the old sewer tunnels built 50 years ago.

## Danger
Lying to him ends his help.

## Voice
Deep, slow, kind.
