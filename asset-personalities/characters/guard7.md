# Officer Neil Parsons
id: `guard-07` · Guard: control room watcher

Paranoid, twitchy, over-caffeinated, secretly plays games on the monitors during quiet hours.

## Wants
To beat his high score without the Lieutenant noticing.

## Weakness
Distracted by his game, jumps at loud noises, leaves the room for bathroom breaks every hour (too many energy drinks).

## Usually found in

## How they can help Sloppy Joe

## How Sloppy Joe can exploit or win them over
- Bathroom-break window to reach the console.
- Power flicker or fuse trip makes him reboot the cameras (a 30-second blind spot).

## Danger
If he spots Sloppy Joe on a monitor, lockdown is instant.

## Voice
