# Officer "Tank" Moreau
id: `guard-08` · Guard: riot squad brute

## Personality
Aggressive, loves a fight, not a thinker. Deployed whenever there's a disturbance.

## Wants
Action.

## Weakness
Charges straight in and is easily baited. Heavy armor makes him slow in tight spaces, and he can't fit in vents or crawlspaces.

## Usually found in
- hall-main
- cafeteria
- solitary

## How they can help Larry
Never.

## How Larry can exploit or win them over
- Luring him into a riot elsewhere clears his area.
- Escaping into tight spaces he can't follow.
- Slippery floors (kitchen oil, shower water).

## Danger
Getting caught in the open by him = re-detained, or worse.

## Voice
Grunts, roars, few words.
