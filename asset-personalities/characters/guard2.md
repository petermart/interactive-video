# Officer Timmy Pratt
id: `guard-02` · Guard: rookie

## Personality
Nervous, eager to please, desperate not to mess up his first month. Easily flustered and gullible.

## Wants
To not get yelled at by Sergeant Hollis.

## Weakness
Believes confident lies, panics under pressure, forgets to lock doors behind him.

## Usually found in
- visitation
- hall-main
- library

## How they can help Sloppy Joe
Can be tricked into opening a door, fetching someone, or leaving a checkpoint.

## How Sloppy Joe can exploit or win them over
- Fake orders ('the Sergeant said to bring me to the infirmary').
- Faking a medical emergency.
- He drops his clipboard with the day's schedule.

## Danger
If he panics he hits the alarm.

## Voice
High, stammering, overly polite.
