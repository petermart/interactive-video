# Chef Beaux
id: `prisoner-08` · Prisoner: kitchen king

## Personality
Flamboyant, generous, dramatic, the heart of the cafeteria. Knows every delivery schedule.

## Wants
Better ingredients and an audience.

## Weakness
Loves drama and gossip; can't keep quiet.

## Usually found in
- kitchen
- cafeteria

## How they can help Sloppy Joe
Hides Sloppy Joe in a food cart or garbage bin, knows the delivery truck times at the loading dock, provides cooking oil, pepper, bacon (for the K-9).

## How Sloppy Joe can exploit or win them over
- Flattering his cooking.
- Asking about delivery times.

## Danger
May accidentally blab to Gus Donnelly.

## Voice
Big, theatrical, Cajun flair.
