# "Hawk"
id: `guard-05` · Guard: tower sniper

## Personality
Silent, patient, cold. Never leaves the tower. Nobody knows his real name.

## Wants
A clean shot, and nothing else.

## Weakness
Only sees what his searchlight and scope point at. Blinded by bright light or smoke, and has a shift change at midnight.

## Usually found in
- guard-tower (always in the cabin)
- perimeter-fence (covers it from the tower)
- rooftop (in view)

## How they can help Larry
Never.

## How Larry can exploit or win them over
- Mirrors or light reflected into the scope.
- Smoke or steam cover.
- Moving during the shift change.

## Danger
Crossing open ground in his searchlight is usually fatal.

## Voice
Rarely speaks; one-word radio replies.
